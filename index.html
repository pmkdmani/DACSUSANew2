Controls Hardware Designer

Industrial electrical and control system design.

View Requirements
  • Knowledge of NFPA, NEC, ISO and NEMA standards.
  • Design of redundant E-Stop circuits, drive circuits, PLC controllers and I/O.
  • Transformer, power supply and wire sizing.
  • Bill of materials and heat calculations.
  • AutoCAD experience required.
  • E-Plan and fluid power design experience are additional strengths.

Controls Software Programmer

Minimum 5 years of experience

View Requirements
  • PLC software design and programming from scratch.
  • Allen-Bradley ControlLogix.
  • Siemens STEP 7, Modicon, Telemechanique, Omron and Mitsubishi.
  • FloPro and SFC experience.
  • Customer-site travel for debugging and installation is required.
